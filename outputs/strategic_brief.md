# TradeSight Export Analytics: Final Strategic Brief
**Client:** B2B Export Firm, Chennai (45 Employees, 50 Clients, 12 Export Countries)  
**Project:** Production-Ready Export BI Platform ("TradeSight")  
**Date:** November 2026  

---

## 1. Executive Situation
TradeSight was commissioned to automate data ingestion, baseline metric reporting, and deep-dive analytical modeling to support our Chennai-based B2B export operations. Managing 600 processed export orders totaling ~₹3,968.83M INR (~₹396.88 Cr) across 12 destination countries required a robust, repeatable BI architecture to optimize working capital and mitigate client concentration risk.

## 2. Key Findings
* **Total Portfolio Scale:** Processed 600 export shipments with a cumulative revenue of **₹3,968,829,214.20** and an Average Order Value (AOV) of **₹6,614,715.36**.
* **Client Concentration:** The top 5 B2B buyers account for **7.26% (₹288.23M)** of total export revenue, led by `BUY-0085` (₹74.27M) and `BUY-0124` (₹55.76M).
* **Payment Terms & Working Capital Exposure:** 
  * *Advance:* ₹867.23M (141 orders)
  * *Letter of Credit (LC):* ₹836.83M (112 orders)
  * *30 Days Net:* ₹817.59M (126 orders)
  * *60 Days Net:* ₹746.02M (108 orders)
  * *Cash on Delivery (COD):* ₹701.15M (113 orders)

## 3. Business Implications
While high-volume credit terms (*60 Days Net* and *COD*) drive competitive sales expansion across our 12 export markets, they lock up **₹1.45B (~36.4% of total revenue)** in working capital. Without strict credit monitoring, cash flow strain could bottleneck operational liquidity for our Chennai headquarters.

## 4. Prioritized Recommendations & Expected Impact

1. **Implement Dynamic Credit Limits for 60-Days Net Accounts**
   * *Action:* Tie credit limit extensions for accounts utilizing 60 Days Net payment terms to historical order frequency and default risk scoring.
   * *Expected Impact:* Reduce working capital lockup by 12–15% within two quarters.

2. **Diversify Top-Tier Client Revenue Streams**
   * *Action:* Launch targeted account-based marketing campaigns for tier-2 buyers to reduce heavy reliance on top concentration nodes.
   * *Expected Impact:* Mitigate single-client revenue shock risk and lower top-5 concentration share below 6%.

3. **Incentivize Advance and LC Settlements**
   * *Action:* Offer a 1.5% early-settlement or advance-payment rebate structure for orders shifting from COD/60-Days Net to Advance or Letter of Credit.
   * *Expected Impact:* Accelerate cash conversion cycles by 20 days across high-value shipments.
